# Mom's Guide to Mother's Cakes 🧁

A friendly, no-jargon guide to using the app on your Samsung phone.

## Putting the app on your phone (one time)

1. Open the link you were given in **Samsung Internet** or **Chrome**.
2. Tap the **menu** (the ⋮ or ≡ in the corner).
3. Tap **"Add page to" → "Home screen"** (or **"Install app"**).
4. Tap **Add**.

You'll now have a **cupcake icon** on your home screen. Tap it any time — it
opens like a normal app and works even without internet. 🎉

## The five buttons at the bottom

| Button | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| 🏠 **Home** | Your dashboard: money made this month and what cakes to bake next. |
| 📋 **Orders** | Every order — who, what cake, when it's due, and how much profit. |
| 🎂 **Cakes** | Your cake recipes and prices. |
| 🧺 **Pantry** | The price of each ingredient (eggs, flour, etc.). |
| ⚙️ **Settings** | Save a backup so you never lose anything. |

## Taking a new order

1. Tap the big **➕ New Order** button (on Home or Orders).
2. Type the **customer's name**.
3. Pick **which cake** and **how many**.
4. Choose the **due date** (when they need it).
5. The **sale price** fills in for you — change it if this order costs more.
6. You'll instantly see the **cost to make** and your **profit**.
7. Tap **Save order**. Done!

## When you're baking an order

1. Tap the order.
2. Under **"What you'll need"** you'll see the exact amounts —
   like *"Eggs: 8, Flour: 600 g"* — already multiplied for the number of cakes.
3. When it's finished, tap **✅ Mark as done**.
4. When they've paid you, tap **💵 Mark as paid**.

## Adding a new kind of cake

1. Tap 🎂 **Cakes → ➕ Add a cake**.
2. Give it a **name**, pick a **little picture**, and set your **selling price**.
3. Tap **+ Add ingredient** for each thing it needs, and type the amount.
4. The app shows your **cost** and **profit** as you go. Tap **Save cake**.

Now that cake is ready to pick whenever you take an order.

## Changing an ingredient's price

Prices change at the store! To update one:

1. Tap 🧺 **Pantry**.
2. Tap the price next to an ingredient and type the new one.
3. That's it — every cake's cost updates automatically.

> The prices that came with the app are typical Walmart prices. Adjust them to
> match what you actually pay.

## ⭐ Very important: back up your data

Your orders live **on this phone**. To keep them safe:

1. Tap ⚙️ **Settings → 💾 Save a backup file**.
2. When the file saves, **email it to yourself** or save it to **Google Drive**.
3. Do this every week or so, and any time you add a lot of orders.

If you ever get a new phone, just install the app, tap
**Settings → 📂 Choose a backup file**, and everything comes back.

## Common questions

**Do I need internet?** No. After you install it, it works offline.

**Will I lose my data if I close the app?** No. It's saved on your phone. Just
keep a backup (above) in case the phone is ever lost or reset.

**Can I use it on my tablet too?** Yes — install it the same way. To copy your
orders over, save a backup on the phone and restore it on the tablet.
